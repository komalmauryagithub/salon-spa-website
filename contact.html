Guest Guidance

Salon Etiquette & Policies

Everything you need to know before stepping into our private sanctuary.

How early should I arrive for my appointment?

We invite you to arrive 10 to 15 minutes before your scheduled service. This allows time to slip into a fresh organic robe and unwind in our lounge with our complimentary seasonal herbal infusion.

Do you offer silent appointments?

Yes. If you prefer to read, work, or quietly rest without conversational dialogue, simply indicate "Silent Appointment" in your booking notes. Your stylist will provide only the necessary consultation and step guidance.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

Because we reserve exclusive chair time for every guest, we kindly request at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or modifications.
