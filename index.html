Certified Master Stylists

Personalized Consultations

The Guest Journey

The Four-Stage Sensory Experience

From the moment you cross our threshold, every sense is calmed. Enjoy artisanal herbal infusions in our ivory reception before transitioning to your private suite.

01

Intimate Diagnostic Dialogue

In-depth discussion covering scalp barrier integrity, hair elasticity, lifestyle habits, and your seasonal hair goals.

02

Micro-Mist Hydrotherapy

Warm ultrasonic steam infused with cedarwood oils opens the hair cuticle, facilitating molecular peptide absorption.

03

Artisanal Cut & Dimensional Tone

Precision sculpting performed with ergonomic Japanese shears, paired with gentle demi-permanent conditioning glosses.

04

Post-Care Prescription

A concise home maintenance protocol tailored to your scalp microbiome, keeping your style pristine between salon visits.
